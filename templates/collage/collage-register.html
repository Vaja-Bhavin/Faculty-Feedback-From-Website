College Registration Guide

Register your college to start using the Faculty Feedback System.

Step 1 - Send Registration Request

Send an email to:

vajabhavin96@gmail.com

Please include the following details:

  • College Name
  • College ID / Institute Code
  • Principal or Authorized Person Name
  • Official College Email Address
  • Contact Number
  • Complete College Address
  • University / Board Affiliation
  • Official Website (if available)
  • Number of Departments (Optional)
  • Any additional information for verification

Step 2 - Verification

After receiving your request, we will verify the submitted information. If additional details are required, we will contact you through your registered email or phone number.

Step 3 - Approval

Once verification is successful, your college will be added to the Faculty Feedback System.

  • College account will be activated.
  • Faculty registration will be enabled.
  • You will receive a confirmation email.

Step 4 - Login

After approval, log in using your registered credentials and start managing your institution.

  • Manage Faculty
  • Allow Faculty Registration
  • Allow Student Feedback
  • View Feedback Reports
Note: Only verified educational institutions are approved. Registration requests with incomplete or invalid information may be rejected.
